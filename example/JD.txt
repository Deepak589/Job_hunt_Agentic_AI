What You'll Build

You will contribute to expanding AI features of SAP BTP Kyma runtime, that means developing multi-agent systems, new skills and tools for agents. All to ease and automate the work of engineers working with SAP BTP Kyma runtime.

Your Daily Tasks Will Include

* Working on large-scale distributed systems in technologies including Kyma (Kubernetes runtime), AI libraries and frameworks like LangGraph, LangChain, LangFuse, etc.
* Developing new AI features, maintaining and improving live AI products and tools for SAP BTP Kyma Runtime
* Working on all aspects of software lifecycle: CI/CD pipelines, automation, testing, releases, etc.

What You Bring

* You have a good grasp of AI concepts such as: agent skills and tools, MCP, A2A, prompt techniques, context-injection, RAG, etc.
* You experimented, worked, or have personal projects employing multi-agent or generative AI frameworks, e.g., LangChain, CrewAI
* You experimented with running different local LLMs, open-source coding harnesses, your own RAG system or similar
* You have experience with Python (REST APIs, FastAPI etc), K3D/S, and understand engineering practices
* You are familiar with orchestration tools: Kubernetes, GitHub Actions
* You adapt quickly to changing technologies, frameworks and communicate clearly
* Ability to work from our Garching bei München office once a week

Where you belong

We are SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) Core team, the heart of SAP’s cloud offering. We build fully distributed, highly scalable, microservices-based architecture leveraging standard OSS Cloud Native Foundation technologies. We offer our customers a modern way to extend and integrate their applications with the cloud-native world. We form the foundation for all extensions and integrations of all SAP systems. We value the entrepreneurial spirit, fostering creativity, and building lasting relationships with our colleagues and the community. Within our team, we practice a trust-based working model.
